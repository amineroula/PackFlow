# Formative 3D brand rules in PackFlow

PackFlow's Formative 3D templates are intentionally locked so packaging and future production documents remain consistent.

## Identity
- Always use the official name **Formative 3D**.
- Use the approved logo without rotating, distorting, outlining, recoloring, adding shadows, changing alignment, or separating the mark from the wordmark.
- Preserve clear space around the logo and place it on a clean, high-contrast background.

## Color palette
- Primary: `#F9423A` Warm Red, `#727562` Olive, `#E3E4E0` Light Taupe.
- Secondary: `#09314A` Dark Blue, `#000000` Black, `#FFFFFF` White.
- Accents: `#4C78FF` Blue, `#FFBC03` Yellow, `#70DAB4` Mint.
- Prefer one accent color per layout/background.

## Typography
- Preferred headline/body font: Elza.
- Preferred technical/accent font: DM Mono.
- PackFlow does not redistribute proprietary fonts. It uses system-safe fallbacks when the brand fonts are unavailable.
- Headlines should be clear and restrained. Technical eyebrows and numbering use mono styling.

## Graphic system
- Premium, clean, technical layouts.
- Rounded cards and expression-shape language.
- Generous negative space.
- Layering texture, when used, must remain subtle and must not reduce legibility.

## Imagery
- Product/manufacturing imagery should feel authentic, clear, and useful.
- Favor strong angles, clean tones, and negative space.
- Avoid busy backgrounds and unnecessary graphic overlays on photos.

## Writing
- Use direct, active language.
- Avoid jargon, vague claims, and unnecessary wording.
- Prefer positive, practical instructions that help the operator complete the task quickly.
